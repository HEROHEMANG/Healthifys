Privacy Policy

App: Healthify
Package name: com.edu.healthify
Effective date: 16.11.2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hemang Singh handles information when you use the Healthify Android app (com.edu.healthify).

Information Healthify accesses and collects

Healthify provides nutrition and wellness information. You can use the app without creating an account or providing your name, email address, phone number, or personal health information. Healthify does not access Health Connect, medical records, or personal fitness measurements.

When the app loads content from our server, the server receives your IP address as part of the request. Our hosting provider may record your IP address, the requested page or resource, and the time of the request in server logs.

If you email us for support, we receive your email address, your message, and any information you choose to include.

How we use and share information

We use server request information to deliver app content, diagnose errors, and protect the service. Our hosting provider processes that information to operate the server.

We use support emails to respond to your inquiries. Our email provider processes those messages to deliver and store them.

Healthify does not use advertising or analytics SDKs. We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising.

Data security

The app connects to our server using HTTPS. Access to server logs and support emails is restricted to authorized personnel.

Data retention and deletion

Server logs are kept for 30 days. Support emails are kept for 30 days.

To request access to or deletion of personal information you sent us, email singhhemang92@gmail.com. We may ask for enough information to verify your request.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Healthify handles information, we will update this page and revise the effective date above.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Hemang Singh at singhhemang92@gmail.com.
